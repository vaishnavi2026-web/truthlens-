# TruthLens Decision Points

## DP1 — Feed Order
TruthLens orders the public feed by risk first and recency second. High-risk claims appear first so potentially harmful misinformation receives attention quickly.

## DP2 — Visibility
Unverified claims remain publicly visible with a clear UNVERIFIED badge. This supports transparency while showing that the claim has not yet been reviewed.

## DP3 — Editing
Submitted claim text cannot be edited after submission. Corrections should be submitted as a new claim to preserve the original submission and review history.
